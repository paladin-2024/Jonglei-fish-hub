# Jonglei Fish Hub — Product Context

## Product Purpose

Admin console for managing the Jonglei Fish Hub: a fish trade platform connecting fish traders, buyers, transporters, and drivers across Jonglei state, South Sudan. Administrators monitor registrations, review orders, track shipments, and manage platform health.

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: platform administrators and government trade officials in Juba and regional offices (Bor, Malakal, Renk). Secondary: market supervisors checking trader registrations. Tertiary: finance staff reviewing transaction reports.

Users are in task — reviewing lists, approving accounts, resolving disputes. They are not browsing. The interface must be dense, navigable, and fast to scan.

## Brand

- **Name:** Jonglei Fish Hub
- **Tone:** Authoritative, clear, purpose-built. Not playful or decorative.
- **Colors:** Teal primary (`#0F766E`), teal-tinted near-white background (`#F4FAF9`), dark teal sidebar
- **Typography:** Outfit for headings and brand text; JetBrains Mono for numbers, IDs, phone numbers, and data

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream dashboards (Vercel/Linear pale gray)
- Glassmorphism or blur-card decorations
- Gradient text
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text
- Heavy animation that interrupts task flow

## Strategic principles

1. Density over whitespace — users are in task, not browsing
2. Earned familiarity — standard patterns (top bar, side nav, data tables) are features
3. State completeness — every component must handle loading, empty, and error
4. Teal palette is fixed — it is the regional/brand color, not arbitrary
